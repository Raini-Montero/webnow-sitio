## Brand & Style

This design system targets ambitious Chilean and Latin American enterprises seeking world-class digital transformation, bespoke software, and revenue-driven growth. The brand voice balances technical authority with executive commercial appeal: confident, sophisticated, agile, and forward-looking. 

The aesthetic merges **Modern Corporate Precision** with refined **Glassmorphism** and vibrant energy. High-contrast slate neutrals provide foundational depth, while electric indigo and cyan illuminate key interactive anchors. Interfaces feature polished micro-interactions, controlled luminosity, clean architectural layouts, and purposeful negative space that instill institutional trust while driving conversion.

## Colors

The palette operates primarily in an elevated, executive dark mode with crisp high-contrast breakout sections.

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Electric Cobalt drives focus, active CTA states, key badges, and interactive focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`)**: Vivid Cyan serves as the hyper-tech companion, applied to metric highlights, glow backdrops, and gradient terminals.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316`)**: Vibrant Coral is reserved strictly for high-conversion signals: live lead triggers, promotional callouts, and conversion urgency hooks.
- **Neutral Foundation**: Deep obsidian slate (`#0F172A`) anchors root views; elevated cards leverage medium slate (`#1E293B`); text utilizes crisp white (`#F8FAFC`) with muted support (`#94A3B8`). Alternating light panels leverage `#F8FAFC` background with `#0F172A` text to generate dynamic contrast shifts between case studies and technical capabilities.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate hierarchy of modern agency prowess. **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers sharp geometric titles that project technical acumen and commercial energy. **Inter** handles narrative readability across complex technical case studies, data sheets, and multi-paragraph service offerings. 

Display headlines apply negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a sculpted, editorial impact. Eyebrow labels use `label-sm` with full uppercase styling and extended letter spacing (`0.06em`) to frame major narrative transitions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content width of 1280px, with edge-to-edge full-bleed background containment for contextual transitions.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutter, 4rem canvas margin. Multi-column service showcases, split hero sections, and asymmetrical metric presentations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.5rem gutter, 2rem canvas margin. Cards consolidate into 2-column matrices; horizontal navigation shifts to sheet-based access.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutter, 1.25rem canvas margin. Layout collapses to vertical linear streams; primary conversion bars dock to bottom safe areas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered opacity, chromatic glows, and frosted glass planes rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Surface Layering**: The primary viewport floor rests at `#0F172A`. Content containers elevate to `#1E293B` with a 1px border perimeter tinted with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Glassmorphic Navigation & Overlays**: Sticky headers and floating controls utilize `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` backed by an aggressive `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle bottom border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Luminous Ambient Backdrops**: High-value focal points (hero conversion cards, pricing pillars, lead generation docks) cast diffused radial gradients using `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` and `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)` with blur radii from 48px to 96px.
- **Shadow Scale**:
  - *Resting card*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`
  - *Interactive hover*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)` accompanied by a border color shift to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sleek contemporary software polish with friendly commercial approachability. Standard components (inputs, primary buttons, chips) adhere to 0.5rem (`rounded-md`). Major containment surfaces—such as service matrices, quotation modules, and testimonial panels—escalate to 1rem (`rounded-lg`) and 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`), creating nested geometric harmony without childish over-rounding. Circular geometry is reserved strictly for avatar rings, iconography badges, and floating status pings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` background, `#F8FAFC` text, `font-weight: 600`, 0.5rem radius, horizontal padding 1.5rem, vertical padding 0.75rem. Hover state triggers a subtle scale shift (`scale(1.02)`) and lightens the fill to `#3B82F6` with a cyan-tinted rim highlight.
- **High-Conversion Tertiary**: Background `#F97316`, white text, paired with subtle pulse animation for instant quote and audit CTAs.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, white text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with border color shifting to `#06B6D4`.

### Header / Navbar
Sticky bar floating with a 1rem margin on desktop, styled with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`, 16px blur, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and 1rem radius. Contains agency mark, service navigation anchors, language switcher (ES/EN), and a compact primary CTA button.

### Cards & Service Containers
Cards feature `#1E293B` backgrounds, 1.5rem corner radii (`rounded-xl`), internal padding of 2rem, and fine borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Hover states elevate the container via a 4px upward translation and illuminate the border using an indigo-to-cyan gradient border effect.

### Input Fields & Selects
Field backgrounds leverage `#0F172A` set inside `#1E293B` containers, padded at 0.75rem vertical and 1rem horizontal, with a 0.5rem radius. Placeholder text rests at `#64748B`. Focus triggers a 1px border in `#2563EB` along with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)` glow ring.

### Chips & Badges
Compact pill markers with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding, using `label-sm` typography. Status tags use semi-transparent fills: Indigo (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` with `#60A5FA` text) and Teal/Cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)` with `#22D3EE` text).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
Custom 18px boxes with 4px border radius for checkboxes and full circular radius for radios. Resting border uses `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Selected state fills with `#2563EB` and displays a crisp white SVG checkmark.

### Conversion Anchors (Audit & Calculator Modules)
Interactive project-estimator containers featuring dynamic slider controls, highlighted milestone outputs styled in `#06B6D4`, and instant form submission drawers to optimize inbound pipeline capture across Santiago's enterprise sector.
